BudgetBuddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information BudgetBuddy handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you when you use the BudgetBuddy mobile app or web app.

In short: BudgetBuddy uses information you provide to manage budgets, secure accounts, and support budget sharing. The web app stores information in your browser. The mobile app can sync account and budget data with BudgetBuddy's API.

1. Information BudgetBuddy handles

BudgetBuddy handles information you enter or provide while using the app, including:

  • Account information: your email address and password. The account service stores a one-way password hash rather than your plain-text password.
  • Budget and transaction information: budget names, item descriptions, planned and paid amounts, dates, archived budget history, and transaction details you choose to record.
  • Sharing information: the email address of a person you invite, invitation status, and budget information made available when a budget is shared.
  • Authentication information: a sign-in token kept on your device so the app can maintain your session.

BudgetBuddy does not connect to your bank or retrieve financial account data. Budget details are information you choose to enter.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, including creating and displaying budgets, saving budget history, signing you in, restoring your session, sending password-reset messages, and processing budget invitations. Budget information may be used to calculate totals and remaining amounts within the app. It is not used to make lending, credit, insurance, or other financial eligibility decisions.

3. Sharing and service providers

BudgetBuddy does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or disclosed in these limited situations:

  • People you invite: when you share a budget, the invited person's email is used to deliver the invitation. A person who accepts can access the shared budget and its contents; only share information you are comfortable making available to them.
  • Service providers: the mobile service uses an API and PostgreSQL database to authenticate accounts and store synced budgets and history. Resend may be used to deliver password-reset and invitation emails when email service is configured.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or another person.

Service providers process information to provide their services, and their own privacy practices may also apply. BudgetBuddy does not currently include in-app advertising or an analytics SDK.

4. Storage, cookies, and security

The web app stores budget, transaction, and invitation data in your browser's local storage. The mobile app stores information on your device; account and budget information may also be sent to the BudgetBuddy API. The API uses HTTPS for app requests. The web app does not require cookies to save local budgets. The server or hosting provider for this page may collect basic request information, such as IP address, browser, and request time, in operational logs.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information, including password hashing and authenticated API access. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

Information remains in browser or device storage until you remove it. Information synced to the service is retained while needed to provide the service or until you delete your account. Operational copies may persist for a limited time in backups or logs.

You can delete your account from the mobile app's main menu or from the account deletion page. Account deletion removes your account, budgets you own, archived budget history, pending password-reset tokens, and invitations addressed to your account from the service. Budgets you own are no longer available to collaborators. Budgets shared with you may remain available to their owners.

Account deletion does not remove local-only web app data from your browser. To remove that data, clear BudgetBuddy's browser storage. To remove locally stored information on a device, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Uninstalling an app does not remove information already synced to the service.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose what information to enter, whether to use budget sharing, and which people to invite. You can sign out of the mobile app to remove its saved sign-in token from that device; this does not delete your account or synced budgets. Delete your account from the main menu to request deletion of your account and associated server-stored information.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Use the in-app account deletion option or contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

7. International processing

Your information may be processed in the country where BudgetBuddy or its service providers operate. Privacy laws in those locations may differ from the laws where you live.

8. Children's privacy

BudgetBuddy is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so it can be reviewed and removed as appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as BudgetBuddy changes or as legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Continued use after an update means this policy applies to your ongoing use, subject to any rights you have under applicable law.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@budgetbuddy.me.
