BudgetBuddy Support

Need help with BudgetBuddy? Contact our support team and include a short description of the issue. Please do not send your password or other sensitive financial information.

Email BudgetBuddy Support

Account access

If you forgot your password, request a password reset using the email address on your account.

Delete your account

You can request permanent deletion of your account and associated server data from the BudgetBuddy account deletion page. Sign in there to confirm and complete your request.

Budget sharing

Budget owners can invite other BudgetBuddy users to share a budget. If you have trouble viewing or responding to an invitation, contact support from the email address associated with your account and describe what happened.

Privacy

Read the BudgetBuddy Privacy Policy to learn how account and budget information is handled.
